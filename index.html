🍿 Fascinating Movie Facts

🎞️ **The First Film Ever Made**: *Roundhay Garden Scene* (1888) is the earliest surviving motion picture, and it's just 2.11 seconds long. Shot in France, it features four people awkwardly walking in a garden. Groundbreaking? Absolutely.

🕰️ **The Longest Film Ever**: *Logistics* (2012) holds the record for being the longest movie ever made — 857 hours (35 days and 17 hours). Spoiler: it’s literally about the production journey of a pedometer.

⚔️ **Lightsaber Secrets**: The legendary Star Wars lightsaber sound was created by combining the hum of an old film projector and the buzzing feedback of a microphone near a TV. Jedi audio wizardry.

🧥 **Hitchcock’s Easter Eggs**: Alfred Hitchcock made cameo appearances in 39 of his 52 films. From missing passengers to newspaper readers, he was *the king of the subtle photobomb*.

🐠 **Finding Dory’s Hidden Detail**: Pixar is famous for hiding characters from their upcoming films in current movies. In *Monsters, Inc.*, you can spot Nemo — three years before *Finding Nemo* was released!

🤯 **The Inception of Inception**: Christopher Nolan wrote *Inception* over 10 years. The dream logic? Based on lucid dreaming, paradoxes, and the golden ratio. No wonder it messed with our heads.
